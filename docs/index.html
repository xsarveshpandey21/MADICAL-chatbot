WHO Dengue Warning Signs & Diagnostic Criteria

Patients diagnosed with Dengue are monitored for clinical warning signs signaling increased risk of severe dengue (Dengue Shock Syndrome / Dengue Hemorrhagic Fever).

Low Platelet Count

< 100,000 cells/µL is the primary threshold for dengue hemorrhagic risk.

Narrow Pulse Pressure

Pulse Pressure (Systolic - Diastolic) ≤ 20 mmHg indicates impending shock.

Clinical Symptoms

Severe Abdominal Pain, Persistent Vomiting, Mucosal Bleeding, Lethargy.

Hematocrit & WBC

Rapid rise in Hematocrit concurrent with rapid decrease in platelet count.

Normal Reference Baselines

Blood Pressure

Systolic: 90 - 120 mmHg

Diastolic: 60 - 80 mmHg

Normal Pulse Pressure: 30 - 50 mmHg

Heart Rate & Respiration

Resting Heart Rate: 60 - 100 bpm

Respiratory Rate: 12 - 20 breaths/min

Body Temp: 36.1 - 37.2 °C (97.0 - 99.0 °F)

Hematology Baseline

Platelets: 150,000 - 450,000 cells/µL

WBC: 4,500 - 11,000 cells/µL

SpO2: 95% - 100%
